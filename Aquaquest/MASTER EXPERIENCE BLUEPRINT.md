# 01. CORE IDEA

## Product

**AquaQuest**

## Concept

AquaQuest is an interactive water-learning experience where users discover how water moves through the planet, homes, bodies, ecosystems, and everyday life.

It combines:

- Educational storytelling
- Interactive exploration
- 3D visual environments
- Short-form learning
- Quizzes
- Progress
- Achievements
- Real-world actions

### The central idea

> **Every drop has a journey.**

The interface should make that journey visible.
