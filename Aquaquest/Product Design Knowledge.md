# AQUAQUEST — Product Design Knowledge

> A modern visual and interactive design system for a water education experience.

## Identity

**Name:** AquaQuest  
**Category:** Interactive Water Education  
**Design Language:** Soft 3D + Playful Science + Modern Editorial UI  
**Core Feeling:** Explore → Discover → Understand → Act

---

## 1. Product Vision

AquaQuest turns water education into an interactive visual journey.

The product should not feel like a textbook or a conventional educational website.

It should feel like:

- A miniature water world
- A premium educational game
- A modern mobile app
- An interactive science museum
- A visual storytelling experience

### Core principle

> **Don't tell users about water. Let them explore water.**

---

## 2. Experience Pillars

### 🌊 Explore

Users discover different aspects of water through visual environments.

### 🔬 Understand

Complex scientific concepts are simplified through animation, diagrams, and interaction.

### 🎮 Play

Quizzes, discoveries, progress, and small interactions make learning rewarding.

### 🌍 Connect

Users understand how water connects:

- People
- Homes
- Nature
- Cities
- Agriculture
- Oceans
- Climate
- The future

### 💧 Act

The experience should eventually answer:

> "What can I do with what I just learned?"

---

# 3. Design Personality

AquaQuest should be:

- Friendly
- Curious
- Optimistic
- Scientific
- Premium
- Playful
- Calm
- Accessible

It should NOT be:

- Babyish
- Cartoonish
- Corporate
- Overly technical
- Visually noisy
- Dark and serious
- Full of generic AI illustrations
