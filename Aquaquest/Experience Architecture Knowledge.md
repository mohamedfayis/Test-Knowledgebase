# AQUAQUEST — Experience Architecture Knowledge

> A complete product knowledge base for designing, rebuilding, and evolving AquaQuest as a modern interactive water-learning experience.

---

## 01. Product Definition

**Product Name:** AquaQuest

**Purpose:**  
An interactive educational experience that helps people understand water through exploration, visual storytelling, interactive science, quizzes, and real-world actions.

### Core proposition

> **Explore water. Understand its journey. Protect its future.**

AquaQuest should transform water education from passive reading into an experience.
